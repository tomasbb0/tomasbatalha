# Launch Your Product!

> "That brain of mine is something more than merely mortal; as time will show."
> (Ada Lovelace, 1844)

Ada died at 36, never seeing her programs run on a real machine. The Analytical Engine was never completed. She had the vision, the skill, and the algorithms, but not the execution. The gap between "it works on my laptop" and "people are using it" is the gap that kills most projects. This part exists so your creations do not share the fate of Ada's. Brilliant, correct, and never shipped.

Code that works is not the same thing as a product someone uses.

This part is about that difference. You have built something, it is working, you have even deployed it... now what? Now you will learn to define what your MVP actually is (hint: it is far less than you think), choose the right channels to distribute your product, and put your creation in the hands of real people.

The goal of this book was never for you to have beautiful code. It was for you to have something someone uses. This is the part where that happens.

**JobPilot progress after this part:** Published on the Chrome Web Store, promoted on Reddit, scope document written, and the gauntlet of Challenge 15 ready for you to prove you can do it all again from scratch.
