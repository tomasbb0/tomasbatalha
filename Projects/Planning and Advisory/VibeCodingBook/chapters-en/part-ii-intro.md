# Tier 2: Builder

> "The science of operations, as derived from mathematics more especially, is a science of itself, and has its own abstract truth and value."
> (Ada Lovelace, 1843)

Ada realised something that her contemporaries missed: computing was not just about numbers. It was about operations: patterns of logic that could be applied to anything. Music, text, images. She saw the general-purpose machine inside a calculator. That leap of abstraction is what separates someone who copies code from someone who builds products.

You can talk to AI. Now you want to build something real.

In this part, you will leave copy and paste behind and step into platforms that generate full applications for you. Lovable, Bolt, v0, Base44, vibecode.com. You describe what you want, the platform generates the code, you refine and deploy. No IDE, no terminal, no manual configuration.

But pressing buttons is not enough. You will learn what happens underneath: how the browser processes things, where data lives, what happens when a user clicks a button. Because if you do not understand what the platform is doing for you, when something goes wrong (and it will), you will not know where to start.

By the end of this part, you will have a real web application with persistent data and users... built entirely with platforms.

**JobPilot progress after this part:** You'll understand why JobPilot can't be built on a platform (and what can), how the browser works under the hood, where JobPilot stores user profiles, and how HTML, CSS, and JavaScript come together in the popup.
