# Tier 3: Master

> "I do not believe that my father was (or ever could have been) such a Poet as I shall be an Analyst; for with me the two go together indissolubly."
> (Ada Lovelace, letter to her mother, 1843)

Ada did not separate creativity from rigour. She was both poet and mathematician, and she argued that the combination was her strength. The best vibe coders are the same: creative enough to imagine what should exist, rigorous enough to understand why it works. In this tier, you stop delegating architecture to platforms and start making your own decisions.

Full control. No limits. No ceiling.

In this part, you will install VS Code or Cursor and work with AI assistants directly in your editor. The AI does not decide the project structure. You do. TypeScript, React, Git, APIs, browser extensions, architecture. You will understand every piece and decide how they fit together.

Why take this step? Because platforms are brilliant until the moment you need something they do not offer. And that moment... always arrives. In Tier 3, AI still does the heavy lifting, but you have control over every file, every decision, every line of code. When something breaks, you will know exactly where to look.

After this part, you will be able to build whatever you want.

**JobPilot progress after this part:** The full architecture is in place. Content scripts reading forms, a service worker calling OpenAI with structured outputs, TypeScript interfaces defining the user profile, message passing connecting all the pieces, Git tracking every change, and the CRXJS build pipeline bundling it all.
