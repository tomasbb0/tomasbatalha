# Tier 1: Apprentice

> "The Analytical Engine weaves algebraical patterns, just as the Jacquard loom weaves flowers and leaves."
> (Ada Lovelace, 1843)

Ada never touched a computer. The machine she wrote programs for had not even been built yet. She studied Charles Babbage's blueprints, understood the logic, and wrote the world's first algorithm on paper. She was the original vibe coder: someone who saw what a machine could do and described the instructions to make it happen, without writing a single line of what we now call code.

You are about to do the same thing. Not on paper, but in a chat window.

Your first programming tool is not a code editor. It is a conversation.

In this part, you will open ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, or any other AI chat, and ask it to write code for you. No installations. No IDE. No terminal. You copy what the AI generates, paste it into a file, and open it in your browser. It sounds too simple... and that is exactly the point. The distance between you and a live website is one well crafted conversation.

You will also learn to read code. Not write it from scratch, but look at what the AI gave you and understand what is there. By the end, you will deploy something real, with a URL you can share with anyone.

If you have never written a line of code in your life, this is precisely where you start.

**JobPilot progress after this part:** You'll have written your first prompt, generated the initial extension files, read the code AI gave you, set up your workspace, and understand what deploying to the Chrome Web Store means.
